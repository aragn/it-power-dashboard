Cross-zonal capacity on the northern border (JAO, Italy North CCR)

The TSOs of Italy, France, Switzerland, Austria and Slovenia compute together how much power may cross each border: the NTC (net transfer capacity). They check every critical network element (a line or transformer) under each contingency (the outage of another element); the one that runs out of margin first is the limiting element. The day-ahead market gets the ATC: the NTC minus the long-term nominations. On top of that, Terna can cap Italy's total imports with an allocation constraint ("special periods"): with low demand and high renewable output, enough Italian plants must stay online to control voltage and keep the system stable, so imports must leave room for them.
